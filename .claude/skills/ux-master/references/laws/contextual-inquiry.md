# Contextual Inquiry

UX Method card.

Related cards in the deck: [User Interview](user-interview.md).

## In one line

A field study that combines observing a small number of users at work with interviewing them, to understand their real practices and behavior.

## The steps

- Introduction: introduce yourself, explain the goal and what to expect, and tell the participant their feedback is confidential.
- Inform: explain that you will watch them work and ask questions when something interesting comes up.
- Explore: observe, and pause to ask open questions about why they did what they did.
- Clarify: at the end, ask what remains and play back your understanding so they can correct it.
- Synthesize: find the patterns and themes across sessions, for example with affinity mapping.

## Reach for it when

- The product supports real work with tools, interruptions and workarounds you cannot see from analytics.
- You are designing for a domain you do not know.
- People's descriptions of their work and the data about it do not match.

## Questions it answers

- What do people do, in what order, with what tools and workarounds?
- What in their environment shapes how they use the product?

## What an agent can and cannot do

An agent can prepare the plan, the observation guide and the note template, and can synthesize real field notes. It cannot observe. Note for deck owners: the printed card carries the Psychology Concept label on its front. It is a research method and is filed with the methods here.
