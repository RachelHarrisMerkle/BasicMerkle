# Design Principles

UX Method card.

Related cards in the deck: [Affinity Mapping](affinity-mapping.md).

## In one line

A short, agreed set of guidelines that frames how a team approaches and settles design problems.

## The steps

- Rally the team: involve as many of the people who will use the principles as you can, so they are adopted.
- Define the criteria: agree what makes a principle useful, for example specific, focused on user needs and able to scale, and what it should cover.
- Diverge: each person writes as many candidate principles as they can in a short, timed burst.
- Converge: share, group into themes and vote on the themes that matter most.
- Refine and apply: test the principles against real or hypothetical work, merge and reword until each is clear, and plan how they will be shared and used.

## Reach for it when

- Design reviews turn on taste, with no shared basis for decisions.
- A new product or team needs a common direction.
- You want each principle backed by a law, which is what the deck suggests: pair each design principle with the UX theory that explains why it works.

## Questions it answers

- What do we optimize for when two good options conflict?
- Which laws matter most for this product and these users?

## What an agent can and cannot do

An agent can run the structure, draft candidate principles from existing decisions and research, pair each principle with its law and stress-test the wording. The team has to own the choice. Principles written by an agent and never debated will not be followed.
